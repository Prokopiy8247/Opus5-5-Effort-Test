# DEVELOPMENT — Horizon Drift: Four Valleys

An original open-world arcade-sim racing game made with Godot 4.7.2 (Forward+, D3D12, Jolt Physics) and GDScript.

## Run
```
godot --path .                                  # play
godot --path . -- --shots=<abs dir>             # screenshot tour
godot --headless --path . -- --drivetest        # handling numbers -> drivetest_results.md
godot --path . -- --lap [--kmh=160]             # autopilot full-loop perf run
godot --headless --path . -- --functest         # events / save / weather checks
godot --headless --path . --script tools/gen_cars.gd   # regenerate data/cars/*.tres
python tools/bl.py code tools/blender_cars.py   # rebuild + export vehicles (Blender on 9877)
python tools/bl.py code tools/blender_kit.py    # rebuild + export prop kit
```

## Controls
Keyboard and mouse only (no gamepad support, by request). W/S: throttle and brake (hold brake at a standstill to reverse). A/D: steer. Space: handbrake. Z/X: look left/right. Q/E: shift (when the gearbox is manual). C: camera. B: look back. Right mouse button: free look. H: horn. L: lights. R: reset to road. F1/Tab: garage. F2: debug menu. `: console. M: map (click to set a waypoint). P: photo mode. F12: screenshot.

## Architecture
- `scripts/world/world_gen.gd`: deterministic world. Region weights, height function (land → water carving → road carving), 13 roads (Catmull-Rom → 4 m samples, smoothed heights with grade limits, junction snapping, crossing pinning), tunnel and bridge flags, and a 4 m road raster (distance, height, road, sample) used for carving, surfaces, events and dressing.
- `road_builder.gd`: road ribbons with banking, shoulders and skirts (render + ConcavePolygonShape3D with `surface` metadata). Also bridges (pillars, rails), tunnel shells and portals, and MultiMesh furniture.
- `terrain_streamer.gd`: 128 m chunks generated on WorkerThreadPool. LOD0 (2 m, HeightMapShape3D collision, vegetation MultiMeshes) within 2 chunks; LOD1 (8 m) within 7 chunks; a 32 m far mesh covers the whole map. Commits are capped at 2 per frame.
- `props.gd`: settlements and POIs from the Blender building kit, pushed clear of roads.
- `sky_weather.gd`: sun and moon, procedural sky, fog and volumetric fog, rain GPUParticles, `wetness` global shader uniform, and the grip factor.
- `vehicle/car.gd`: project-owned raycast vehicle on RigidBody3D (see below). Also `chase_cam.gd`, `car_audio.gd`, `skid_marks.gd`.
- `traffic/traffic_manager.gd`: pooled frozen-kinematic RigidBody3D traffic on lanes.
- `events/event_manager.gd`: traps, zones, drifts, jumps, races, time trial.
- `ui/`: HUD, minimap and map, console, garage and debug menus. `autoload/game.gd`: input map, car database, save/load.
- `qa/autotest.gd`: command-line QA harness.

## Vehicle model: why a raycast RigidBody3D
VehicleBody3D (also available under Jolt) offers only a simple friction-slip model and gives little control over combined slip, assists or surface grip. I chose a project-owned model:
- 4 rays from the suspension mounts; spring stiffness comes from the ride frequency, and damping from the damping ratio. Anti-roll bars act per axle.
- Tyres: lateral force `μ·N·tanh(slip/α)`, with longitudinal drive and brake forces and a friction ellipse for combined slip (power oversteer on RWD). Grip is per surface, per weather condition and for offroad.
- Engine torque curve from rated power; automatic box with throttle-dependent shift points. FWD/RWD/AWD split. The drag coefficient is solved so drag balances power at the rated top speed. Downforce is k·v².
- Assists: TCS caps drive force at grip, ABS caps brake force, STM damps yaw-rate error, steering assist adds counter-steer.
- Physics runs at 120 Hz with CCD on the body; there is air-control torque for landings, and `reset`/`flip` commands.

## Map layout
4 × 4 km, centred at the origin. NW: Verdance Vale (farmland, river, village). NE: Highpine Ridge (mountains, switchbacks, tunnels, snow peaks). SE: Ochre Mesa (mesas, dirt trails, gas station, diner). SW: Saltwind Coast (cliffs, bay bridge, harbour, lighthouse, wind farm). The Grand Valley Loop (20.2 km) passes through all four regions; the Crossland Highway (3.3 km, 4 lanes) crosses E–W.
