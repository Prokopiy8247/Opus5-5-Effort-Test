# VALORIA: Open Road Festival — development notes

Original open-world arcade-sim racing game for Godot 4.7 (Forward+, Jolt Physics, GDScript).
Forza Horizon 5 was used only as a mechanics/scope reference (see `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`); every
name, model, sound, texture and UI element here is original and project-generated.

## 2. Running the game
- **Standalone build (no Godot needed):** run `exports\windows\VALORIA.exe` (single 160 MB file, data
  pack embedded, the baked world included, so the game starts straight into free roam). Rebuild it
  with `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/export_windows.ps1` (add `-SelfTest` to launch
  the fresh exe and print its status line). The preset is `export_presets.cfg` → "Windows"
  (Windows Desktop, x86_64, release template 4.7.2, embedded PCK; QA, Blender and doc files excluded).
  Saves, screenshots and logs go to `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\VALORIA- Open Road Festival\`.
  `VALORIA.exe -- --selftest=30` plays for 30 s, writes `selftest.png` and a `SELFTEST` status line to
  the log there, and quits. Export outputs are git-ignored.
- **Play from the editor:** open the folder in Godot 4.7 and press F5, or run
  `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path "<project>"`. The main scene is `res://scenes/main.tscn`.
- **First launch** generates the world (`res://world/generated/world_data.res`, ~17 MB, about 30 s on a
  worker thread with a progress screen). It is deterministic (seeded) and git-ignored; regenerate
  explicitly with `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/world_bake.gd`.
- **Project settings / input map** are reproducible: `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/setup_project.gd`.
- **Data resources** (cars, surfaces, upgrades, traffic types, weather): `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/gen_data.gd`.
- **Helper:** `tools/run_godot.ps1 -Timeout <s> -Filter <regex> -A "<godot args>"` runs the console
  executable with a hard timeout and kills only the process tree it started.
- **Command-line options** (after `--`): `--car=<id>`, `--fresh` (ignore the save), `--test-track`,
  `--selftest[=<s>]` (build check, also in the exe), `--qa=<script>` (runs `tools/qa/<script>.gd` with
  a watchdog; editor/project runs only), `--qa-timeout=<s>`.
- **Export-safe loading:** data folders are enumerated with `ResourceLoader.list_directory` (works
  for remapped/imported files inside a .pck); a world generated by an exported build is saved to
  `user://world_data.res` because `res://` is read-only there.

Central managers own their per-frame work (traffic, activities, audio, dressing, streaming); world
objects are MultiMeshes or static geometry, not per-node scripts.

### 4.1 World generation and streaming
- `tools/world/world_layout.gd` holds the hand-designed layout: road control points (with switchback
  "ladders"), peaks, mesas, canyon, coast, beaches, river, POIs, settlements and races.
- `WorldBaker` evaluates region-weighted natural terrain at 4 m (upsampled to 2 m, 2049² grid over
  4096 m), carves the river and coastline, builds Catmull-Rom roads with meanders, computes grade-limited
  elevation profiles (Lipschitz envelope, per-class limits 7–16 %), class-scaled vertical smoothing and
  junction pins, classifies crossings (overpass/underpass), bridges (fill > 9 m or water) and tunnels,
  carves cuttings/embankments with road-core ownership, clips terrain at bridge/tunnel ends, cuts tunnel
  portal holes (NaN heights), and places buildings (anchor buildings such as the church, shop, diner
  and fuel stations first, with a full candidate scan), ~59k trees/rocks, activities and the spawn.
- `TerrainStreamer` streams 256 m chunks in three LODs, built on `WorkerThreadPool`; `HeightMapShape3D`
  collision only near the player. Road meshes, verges, rails, bridges and tunnels are built per road
  piece in a group task; collision is `ConcavePolygonShape3D` with `surface`/`road` metadata.
- Vegetation is one MultiMesh per (cell, species, LOD): 256 m cells for trees, 128 m for small plants,
  hard LOD switch at 180–330 m and far culling at 0.4–2 km (cell-centred ranges, so no whole-cell
  dithering). Trunk/boulder colliders are created only around the player. Grass clumps are scattered
  around the camera on a worker thread.

### 4.2 Vehicle model (why a project-owned raycast vehicle)
`CarController` is a `RigidBody3D` with four raycast/spring wheels at 120 Hz: per-wheel suspension with
bump/rebound damping and anti-roll bars; wheel spin integrated in 10 sub-steps; combined-slip tyre model
(sin(C·atan(B·s)) shape, load sensitivity, per-surface and wet grip multipliers, slip-velocity floor);
engine torque curve, clutch slip at launch, automatic gearbox (ground-speed decisions, part-throttle
short-shifting, kick-down, anti-hunting guards) or manual; open/LSD/AWD centre coupling (opens on
handbrake for drifts); aero drag and downforce; TCS/ABS/stability/steer assists; air control and
auto-flip; tunnelling guards (continuous collision on the body, thick road collision).

The same car was rebuilt as a `VehicleBody3D` prototype with identical mass, centre of mass, wheel
positions and torque curve (`tools/qa/vehiclebody_compare.gd`, `qa/results/vehiclebody_compare.json`):
it accelerated 0–100 km/h in 3.19 s and stopped in 20.8 m, but the 200 km/h lane change spun it
(120.6° body slip, ended upside down) and a handbrake turn did not recover (179.9°). The raycast car is
stable in the same tests (below), so it was kept.

### 4.4 Traffic
Lane graph from the baked road samples (right-hand traffic, per-class lane counts and speed limits,
junction nodes via union-find; `RoadGraph` also drives GPS routing with Dijkstra). `TrafficManager`
keeps a pool of 36 cars (target ~26 near the player, scaled by the density setting and time of day),
spawns outside the camera view, uses IDM car-following (gap, headway 1.4 s), curvature speed limits,
give-way and node reservation at junctions, and three simulation levels: full physics within ~125 m
(PD-tracked to a lane "ghost" so collisions are real), kinematic between 155–650 m, despawn beyond.
Collisions → physics response, then recover or stop, horn/flash feedback; stuck cars despawn. Six
Blender-built types: compact, estate, van, lorry, bus, tractor (tractors only on farm/rural roads).

### 4.5 Activities
20 baked activities: 5 speed traps, 2 speed zones, 3 drift zones, 4 danger-sign jumps (Blender kicker
with convex collision, placed beside the road), 5 checkpoint races and the 20 km loop time trial.
Traps/zones/jumps start by driving through them; races and the time trial start with Enter at the arch
(car placed on the line, 3-2-1 countdown, GPS line to the next checkpoint, abandon with Enter or by
leaving the route). Results show a banner, update personal bests and award festival credits.

### 4.6 Audio (all procedural)
- Engine: per-profile wavetable of one engine cycle (one exhaust pulse per cylinder with seeded
  jitter: i4, i4 turbo, i4 rally, vintage i4, v6, v6 turbo, cross-plane v8, truck v8, flat-plane v8,
  v12), on-load/off-load tables blended by load, played through an `AudioStreamGenerator` at the
  RPM-derived firing rate with filtered noise and soft saturation. `tools/qa/audio_test.gd` renders all
  profiles at 900/3500/6500 rpm: 30/30 within an octave of the expected firing frequency (26/30 exact),
  no clipping; reference renders in `qa/audio/`.
- Generated samples (worker thread, ~1.7 s at start): turbo whistle and blow-off, shift clunk, tyre
  squeal (paved), gravel crunch and grass roll (loose), wind, rain, suspension thump, impact, horn,
  thunder, UI chime, traffic engine hum (three nearest cars) and regional ambience loops (valley birds,
  highland wind, desert wind/cicadas, coast surf/gulls, night crickets).

### 4.7 Presentation and UI
Custom sky shader (sun, moon, stars, clouds), day/night cycle (1 game hour per real minute), five
weather presets (clear, cloudy, rain, fog, storm) blended smoothly with wetness (global shader uniform)
and grip, rain/snow particles, lightning, AgX tone mapping. HUD (speedometer km/h or mph, tachometer,
gear, surface and assist indicators), rotating minimap with roads/POIs/activities/traffic/GPS line,
full map with regions/places/activities/route, region popups, notifications, garage (10 cars with
stats, paint with finishes/stripes/rims, upgrades with stat deltas, tuning), console, debug/benchmark
menu, photo mode (free camera, FOV, depth of field, time/weather, hide panel, screenshots).

## 5. Map
4 × 4 km island, four regions plus a festival hub:

| Region | Character | Landmarks |
|---|---|---|
| Verdana Valley (centre/east) | rolling farmland, forests, river with bridges | Valoria Festival Grounds (hub), Bellacqua village, Sunfield and Orchard farms, Rocca Vecchia castle ruins, Expressway diner, Valley Fuel |
| Pinecrest Highlands (north-west) | climbing pass with switchback ladders, conifer forest, snowy peak | Pinecrest Pass, Summit viewpoint, Alpe Rosa hamlet |
| Redmesa Canyons (south-west) | desert plateau, mesas, canyon trail, cacti | Redmesa Fuel Stop, Canyon Overlook |
| Marisol Coast (east) | cliffs, beaches, sea, wind farm | Porto Lume harbour, Capo Faro lighthouse, Marisol wind farm, Marisol beach |

Roads: 40.04 km in total over 19 roads — the **Festival Loop** (20.05 km, closed, through all four
regions), the **Valoria Expressway** (5.04 km dual carriageway), valley road with a tunnel, summit
road, canyon trail (gravel), mesa ridge, coastal cliffs, vineyard and farm roads, dirt tracks and
spur roads to POIs. Long uninterrupted stretches: the loop itself and the 5 km expressway. 47 bridges
(5.29 km in total) are generated where fills exceed 9 m or cross water (concrete decks, parapets, piers,
abutments); the expressway crosses the loop twice on overpasses; the valley road has a 602 m tunnel.
Settlements hold 49 buildings (houses, church, shop, chalets, barns, farmhouses, silos, sheds,
boathouse, two fuel stations, diner) plus 11 landmark instances. Road furniture: lane markings, shoulders, verges,
guard rails, reflector posts, speed-limit/chevron/warning/direction signs, street lamps in villages.

## 6. Blender workflow
- Routing: only the MCP server on `localhost:9880` (`blender-opus55-xhigh`); every build script starts
  with `vgk_common.guard()`, which compares `bpy.data.filepath` with the project master and aborts
  otherwise. The original master was backed up before the first modification
  (`backups/blender/Opus55ForzaXhighEffort.original-20261023-191638.blend`, git-ignored).
- `tools/blender/car_builder.py` + `car_specs.py`: parametric cars from a station lattice (pchip
  profiles for roof, belt, sills, plan width), subdivision with creases, boolean wheel arches, projected
  decals (lights, grilles, vents, plates), revolved tyres/rims with five spoke styles, calipers and
  discs, interior shell with seats/dash/steering wheel, mirrors, wings, exhausts, sockets for cameras.
  Run inside Blender through the MCP: `exec(open(bpy.path.abspath("//tools/blender/run_build.py")).read()); build([...], export=True)`.
- `tools/blender/kit_builder.py`: 42 world pieces — vegetation with `_LOD1` meshes (broadleaf, conifer,
  poplar, palm, umbrella pine, cactus, bush, boulder, dry shrub, grass clump), 13 buildings (three
  houses, shop, church, shed, boathouse, chalet, barn, farmhouse, silo, gas station, diner), landmarks
  (lighthouse, castle, festival hub, viewpoint, wind turbine with separate rotor), road kit (bridge
  pier/abutment, rail post, reflector post, street lamp, tunnel portal with boolean-cut profile, four
  sign types) and activity props (jump ramp, speed camera, zone banner, race arch).
- Export: glTF binary (+Y up), one file per car/piece into `assets/imported/`; Godot maps material
  names to project materials (`CarMaterials`, `KitMaterials`), so paint, emissive lights and night
  windows are controlled at runtime.
- Visual QA: renders through the MCP `look` tool and Workbench renders of isolated pieces, then
  in-game screenshots (`tools/qa/screens.gd`, `fx_test.gd`).

## 8. Performance (measured with `tools/qa/perf_test.gd`, RTX 5070 / Ryzen 7 5700X3D, VSync to a 180 Hz display)
| Run | Avg FPS | p50 / p95 / p99 frame | Worst frame | Frames > 33 ms | Memory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1920×1080, 10 min autopilot on the Festival Loop with traffic | 157.0 | 5.56 / 11.11 / 15.99 ms | 53.8 ms | 24 of 93,742 | +7.8 MB | no falls; node count varies with tree colliders near the car |
| 1280×720, 3 min, same route | 172.5 | 5.56 / 7.78 / 11.18 ms | 38.9 ms | 2 of 30,542 | −2.1 MB | |
| 1920×1080, Vortex S9 at up to 314 km/h on the expressway | 176.0 | 5.56 / 6.27 / 11.11 ms | 42.4 ms | 3 of 12,675 | +0.6 MB | streaming at top speed |

The 5.56 ms median is the VSync limit of the 180 Hz display, i.e. the game normally runs faster than
the display. Draw calls ~750–1,500 and 0.8–1.8 M primitives per frame with ~25 traffic cars; the 60 FPS
target (and the 30 FPS floor) is met with a wide margin. Single-precision is fine at ±2 km from the
origin (no jitter observed at the map edges), so no origin shifting was added. Results:
`qa/results/perf_*.json`.

## 9. Known limitations
- Gamepad support is intentionally not implemented (user request); keyboard + mouse only.
- Races have no AI opponents (optional stretch in the brief); you race the clock and your best time.
- Visual damage is not modelled; impacts are physical and audible only.
- No collectibles/photo-spot challenges or parked cars.
- Building interiors are not modelled; buildings have box collision.
- The autopilot used by QA and the benchmark is a test tool; it can leave narrow gravel roads at speed.
- Engine sounds are synthetic; heavily filtered profiles (truck V8) can read an octave high.
- Road edges on steep side slopes show the 2 m terrain grid as a saw-tooth when seen from high above.
